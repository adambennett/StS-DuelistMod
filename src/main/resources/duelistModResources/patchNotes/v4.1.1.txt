Content Updates
-------------------


Balance Updates
-----------------
- Colossal Fighter upgrade changed from +3 damage -> -1 Tributes

Bug Fixes
-----------
- Fixed a game crash that occurred when playing Vendread Revolution
- Fixed a game crash that could occur on the custom run menu when enabling Challenge Mode
- Fixed an issue where Curse Royal would continue to trigger even if you had Ectoplasm
- Fixed ConcurrentModificationException that triggered when playing Dust Storm of Gusto

QoL
------
- Obelisk renamed -> Obelisk the Tormentor
- Tier scores are now pulled from 'Overall Score' rather than Act1/Act2/Act3 score depending on your current act
    - This seems to improve score variety among the cards in most pools, especially during Act 1
- Exodia deck no longer applies Soulbound to cards that are already Soulbound
- Fixed tooltip for 'Remove Ojamas from Beast Deck' gameplay option to properly reflect 'enabled by default' instead of the opposite
- Improved handling of custom run modifiers: Shiny, Draft, Sealed, Chimera, Insanity
    - Chimera now starts you with a Duelist-specific custom deck
    - Draft, Sealed, Chimera, Insanity will now also have a chance to replace copies of Sparks with Special Sparks
    - If "Force Special Sparks" config option is enabled, you will now always get a copy, even with any of these custom modifiers enabled
    - Duelist's Bane will now appear in place of Ascender's Bane on high Challenge Level when using any of these custom modifiers
    - These custom modifiers will no longer prevent Magnet cards from being added to your deck via the config option
    - Stanley's Sketchbook will now properly appear in your starting deck when using any of these custom modifiers
    - These custom modifiers will no longer prevent Holiday cards from being added to your deck
    - When playing the Exodia deck, all cards in your starting deck will become Soulbound, even when playing with any of these custom modifiers
